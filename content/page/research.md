I used to work on logical and game-theoretic foundations of multi-agent systems. I did my PhD under the supervision of [Thomas Ågotnes](https://www.uib.no/en/persons/Thomas.%C3%85gotnes) as part of his [LII research group](http://www.uib.no/en/rg/lii). My thesis "Agents that Play by the Rules" was reviewed by [John-Jules Meyer](http://www.cs.uu.nl/staff/jj.html) and [Natasha Alechina](http://www.cs.nott.ac.uk/~nza/) and defended in April 2015.

Before my PhD studies I was a student at [MISH](http://www.mish.uw.edu.pl/), University of Warsaw, Poland, where I graduated with a master's degree in philosophy. My MA thesis was supervised by [Joanna Golińska-Pilarek](https://filozofia.uw.edu.pl/en/pracownik/joanna-golinska-pilarek/).
