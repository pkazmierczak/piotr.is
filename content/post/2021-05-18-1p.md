I always admired [AgileBits](https://agilebits.com/) and I've been a paying
[1Password](https://1password.com/) customer for years. I've also been
recommending people use 1Password over other solutions, and I've often been
asked "why this, why not another solution?" My answer always was: because
1Password is a polished product. Well, today, with the
[announcement](https://blog.1password.com/welcoming-linux-to-the-1password-family/)
of 1Password officially coming to Linux, the company's attention to detail and
quality shines really brightly. Not only have they went the extra mile(s) to
integrate with multiple desktop Linux technologies in order to get the UX
really great, but also they did it for, let's be honest here, a minuscule
financial gain. There can't be more than a couple hundred paying 1Password
Linux users, and yet 1Passowrd delivers premium user experience for the niche
platform.

Chapeau bas, AgileBits!
